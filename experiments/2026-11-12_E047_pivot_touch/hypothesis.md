# E047 Hypothesis: pivot-point R1/S1 first touch, continuation (B56), dev only (pre-registered 2026-11-12 R125, before any run)

- **H1 (B56):** on the dev block, after the first 1-minute EUR/USD bar of a UTC date that touches the classic floor-trader pivot
  resistance R1 from below (c(t-1) < R1 <= h(t)) a long position, and after the first bar that touches support S1 from above
  (c(t-1) > S1 >= l(t)) a short position, entered at t + 1 min and held 120 minutes, earns a mean signed mid return > 1 pip per event.
- **H0:** the mean signed return per event is zero.
- **Mechanism [PUB, weak]:** Johnsson & Frykmer (2019), Lund University master's essay
  ([record](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/record/8979805), [PDF](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/record/8979805/file/8979814.pdf),
  opened R124, ~70% verbatim): traditional pivot trading of EUR/USD wins < 50% (weighted 41.4%, R-only 39.0%, R1 38.5%, S1 45.8% n.s.);
  "profits can be generated by implementing the strategy inversely". Our reading (not the thesis's): stop orders cluster just beyond
  widely watched levels (Osler 2002/2003 logic transferred to pivots), so first touches run through. Sign fixed in R124 before any
  dev pivot statistic.
- **Parent experiment / variable changed:** none (new family: mechanically computed previous-day pivot levels). Touch, fill and
  exclusion machinery reused from E045 (`round_numbers.build_trades`); the hold is 120 minutes (thesis: brackets resolve in "about two
  to three hours").
- **Contamination note:** the thesis's EUR/USD sample (Jun-Dec 2017) is ~10% of our dev dates; the result excluding 2017-06-01 ..
  2017-12-29 is reported separately (descriptive). E045 (round-number rejected-touch fade, NONE, -0.71) is a different level set and
  was not used to fix the sign. No pivot statistic has been computed in this repo. Dev usable.
- **Prior expectation:** weak (non-peer-reviewed thesis, win rates only, no costs; 120-min SD ~15-25 pips makes a 1-pip mean hard to
  reach). ~266 registered trials after this one: a single p just under 0.05 is expected noise (lesson R092), hence B57 val confirmation.
- **Why now:** top (only) `ready` backlog item after the R124 refill; last research-batch run (43155068137, E046) already recorded;
  nothing queued or in progress.
